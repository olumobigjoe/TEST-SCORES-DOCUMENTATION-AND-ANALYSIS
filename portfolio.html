About

Building tools that
make education measurable

I build digital systems that sit at the intersection of education and data. My work starts with a real problem — how do you run a fair, cheat-resistant test for dozens of students simultaneously? — and ends with an analysis of what the results actually mean for teaching.

My recent projects include a randomised, timed online test system for a university instrumentation course, a secure digital voting platform for academic elections, and a comprehensive performance analysis of 65 student records across two test batches.

I am comfortable across the full stack: Python for logic, Streamlit for deployment, Pandas for analysis, and Chart.js or Plotly for visualisation. My documentation is treated as a deliverable, not an afterthought.
